    About BASECRAFT

    Converts integers and fractions between any bases 2–36 using exact BigInt rational arithmetic. Letters A–Z are digits 10–35 (case-insensitive).

    • Enter convert · Ctrl+Enter convert from anywhere in the panel
    • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+C copy result · Esc close dialogs

    Truncated fractions are marked “≈”; converting them back may differ from the original.
